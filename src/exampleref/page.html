YOUR ARTIST BIO
Amelia Hammer Harris is a Los Angeles-based singer, songwriter, producer, and actor making cinematic dark pop with Americana bones. Born in New York City and shaped by a formative move to Kentucky, she carries folk, blues, bluegrass, country, and rock influences beneath a modern sound built from hard drums, shadowy textures, close-up vocals, and sharp melodic pop instinct.
‍
Her current work sits in the territory where BANKS’ nocturnal intensity, Tinashe’s rhythmic precision, and Doja Cat’s irreverent character-play meet Amelia’s own love of trip-hop and theatrical performance. Amelia introduced her solo 2026 catalog with Cheetah Corvette and Bueno, while appearing as Tina in HBO’s Euphoria. Her world is polished, strange, funny, fast, and emotionally close to the surface.
CORE THEMES
New York Theater Kid, Kentucky Roots, Los Angeles Pop Star
Amelia’s story begins with a real collision of worlds: she grew up in New York City’s theater culture before moving to Kentucky, where she found the folk, blues, bluegrass, country, and rock music that shaped her early writing. She later described her own original sound as bluesy and rocky, with country elements, nostalgic textures, modern drums, and trip-hop. That is the core differentiator. Plenty of developing pop artists can access fashion and dark production; far fewer have an authentic line from theater training and Appalachian-rooted influence into sleek, contemporary pop. The next step: use that biographical contrast as creative material—not in a generic “genre-fluid” claim, but in a clear story of where the dark pop actually comes from.

Rina Sawayama
Rina is the reference for fearless genre collision: big melodic pop, rock-adjacent intensity, high-fashion visual theater, and a performer’s ability to turn an emotional song into a full scene. She makes the missing bridge between Amelia’s theatrical training, her rock roots, and her present pop ambition.
